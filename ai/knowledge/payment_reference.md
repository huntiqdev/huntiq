## 2. Detailed Technical & Operational Rules
- Telebirr: Native integration for mobile wallet payments via USSD/SuperApp.
- Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE): CBE Birr and direct bank transfer reconciliation.
- Chapa: Visa, Mastercard, and international diaspora card processing.
- VAT Compliance: All platform fees include 15% Ethiopian Value Added Tax (VAT).
- Zero Commission on Sales: Peer-to-peer sales carry 0% transaction commission.
