## 1. Scope & System Objective
Official support channels and response time commitments for HUNTIQ users.

---

## 2. Detailed Technical & Operational Rules
- Channels: In-app live chat, email (support@huntiq.com), and official phone hotline.
- Operating Hours: Monday through Saturday, 8:30 AM to 6:00 PM East Africa Time (EAT).
- First Response SLA: Within 2 hours for standard tickets; within 15 minutes for payment issues.
