## 2. Detailed Technical & Operational Rules
- Real Estate: ካርታ (Title deed), ፔንሲዮን (Pension/Guest house), ኮንዶሚኒየም (Condominium), ቪላ (Villa), ሰርቪስ ቤት (Service quarter).
- Automotive: ቀረጥ የተከፈለ (Duty paid), ኮድ 2 (Private plate), ኮድ 3 (Commercial plate), ቦሌ (Manual), አውቶማቲክ (Automatic).
- Electronics: ቦክስ ያልተከፈተ (Brand new sealed), ከዱባይ የመጣ (Dubai used import), ኦሪጅናል (Genuine).
- Agriculture: ጤፍ (Teff), ማኛ (Magna teff), ሰርገኛ (Sergegna teff), ቡና (Coffee), ኩንታል (Quintal).
