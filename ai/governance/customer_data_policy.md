## 2. Detailed Technical & Operational Rules
- Transparent Terms: Users are clearly informed that natural language search queries are processed by the HUNTIQ AI assistant.
- Opt-Out Mechanism: Users may request their search history not be utilized in future training corpora.
- No Sensitive Data Collection: Biometric, religious, or political data is never collected or inferred.
